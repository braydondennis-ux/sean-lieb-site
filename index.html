About Sean

Arizona retail, engineered for value.

Sean joined LevRose in 2022 after building his career at SRS and Cushman & Wakefield / DTZ. Today he represents landlords and tenants and closes investment sales throughout the Valley and Arizona's tertiary markets.

He partners with owners on valuation, positioning and lease negotiation — and guides national retailers through site selection and expansion.

The Lieb Family in Phoenix

Three generations of building Phoenix.

Sean's connection to Phoenix runs deeper than one career. The Lieb family has helped shape the city's residential, commercial, and cultural landscape for more than half a century.

"Real estate runs in my family. My grandfather opened the first nightclub in Phoenix. My uncle shaped the commercial market. My father helps the families who actually live here find their homes. I get to represent the national brands that fill the Valley."

Herb Lieb

Sean's grandfather

A dynamic entrepreneur who moved to Phoenix in the 1960s and opened the city's first nightclub, Herb's Underground, followed by The Jockey Club.

Michael Lieb

Sean's uncle

A prominent Phoenix commercial real estate expert and developer who has helped shape the Valley's commercial landscape.

Bobby Lieb

Sean's father

One of Phoenix's best-known residential real estate agents, helping families across the Valley find their homes.

FAQ

Common questions.

What does Sean Lieb specialize in?

Sean is a retail commercial real estate specialist — handling landlord and tenant representation plus investment sales (including shopping centers and single-tenant net-lease assets) across Arizona.

What areas does Sean Lieb serve?

Greater Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tucson, and Arizona's tertiary markets. His office is at 4414 N Civic Center Plaza, Ste 201, Scottsdale, AZ 85251.

Does Sean represent both landlords and tenants?

Yes. Sean represents property owners on leasing, valuation and dispositions, and represents national, regional and local retailers on site selection and lease negotiation — as well as buyers and sellers on investment sales.

How experienced is Sean Lieb?

Over a decade in Arizona retail, with more than $100 million in client transaction revenue and over $60 million in value created. He is a Partner at LevRose Commercial Real Estate / TCN Worldwide and is rated 5.0 on Google.
